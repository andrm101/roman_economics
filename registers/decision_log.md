- **2026-10-24 — D021 — Correction: Morelli-reading whole weighed cargo is c. 213–219 t, not 'c. 195 t'.** The earlier figure added whole pepper (135 t) to valuated (three-quarter) non-pepper goods (~60 t). It is now computed in code (`romeco.m0.throughput.whole_mass_t`, test-pinned). The De Romanis figure (608–624 t) was correct. The mass ratio is 2.85, not 3.2.
- **2026-10-24 — D022 — No documentary traffic count is constructible for the 100–200 CE trade route from open sources.** HGV metadata (idp.data 41a1c87) lists 2,630 Eastern Desert documents. Only 4 of Berenike's 523 and 0 of Myos Hormos's 73 date to 100–200 CE. The 1,421 W2 documents are mostly quarry and fort ostraca, and only 73 are month-dated. Documents give lower bounds on attested activity, never an upper bound, so they cannot make the H1a capacity criterion evaluable. Remaining routes are physical capacity (well yields; Cuvigny et al. 2011 *Didymoi*, not open) or 1st-c. transfer from the Nikanor and O.Berenike archives, with a stated transfer assumption. WP2 is closed as descriptive (D020). Next: WP3, the novelty gate.
- **2026-10-24 — D023 — WP4 corpus pipeline design.**
  - Bibliographic entities stay in `source_register.csv`. File copies, relations and eight independent pipeline states live in `registers/corpus/`.
  - Files are content-addressed (sha256); parsed text is git-ignored.
  - Committed extractions carry snippets only where the rights register permits (test-enforced).
  - Rule-based, versioned extractors rather than an LLM: deterministic, auditable and benchmarkable here.
  - Discovery is offline backward chaining, because Crossref/OpenAlex are unreachable.
  - Reversible: an LLM- or GROBID-based extractor can be added as another versioned extractor and benchmarked on the same gold.
- **2026-10-24 — D024 — Extractions are candidates, never evidence.** On the held-out sample (v0.2 frozen), quantity recall is 0.54 [0.29, 0.77] and citation recall 0.48 [0.29, 0.67]. No extracted value enters the parameter register without direct review of the page image or edition. Pliny's numerals must be read from the printed edition (K22). Absence of an extraction is not evidence of absence in the source.
- **2026-10-24 — D025 — Benchmark discipline.** v0.1 results on the development sample are the registered baseline. v0.2 was tuned on the development errors and is reported there only as a fit. The quoted estimate comes from a held-out sample drawn after v0.2 was frozen. Each later extractor version needs a fresh held-out sample. The gold set is assistant-annotated (not independent) until the researcher reviews `benchmark/gold_review_sheet.csv`.
