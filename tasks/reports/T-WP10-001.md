## Escalations

**E1: Spec ambiguous on join rules (stop condition met).** `docs/expansion/02_ontology_and_data.md` §4 states:

> Overlap join: returns pairs whose intervals overlap, with the overlap fraction.
> Containment join: A is inside B.
> Lag join: B starts within k years after A ends.

Ambiguities that need a researcher decision (for example a D-entry):

1. **Overlap fraction denominator (blocking).** The spec does not say what the fraction is measured against: |A|, |B|, |A ∪ B| (Jaccard), or min(|A|, |B|). Each choice gives a different fraction for the same pair, and the choice also affects the weighting in any downstream correlation. Related: should the join return both directional fractions?
2. **Lag join boundaries (blocking).** Proposed definition: lag = astro(B.t_low) - astro(A.t_high). The spec leaves three things open:
   - (a) Does lag = 0 (B starts in A's final year) count as "after"?
   - (b) Is k inclusive?
   - (c) Are pairs with lag < 0 (B starts before A ends) always excluded, or should they fall under the overlap join?
3. **Interval semantics (affects all three joins).** `t_low`/`t_high` could be either:
   - the duration of an event (it lasted from t_low to t_high), or
   - the uncertainty bounds on a point or short-lived date (it happened once, somewhere in [t_low, t_high]).

   Overlap and containment mean different things under each reading. Under the uncertainty reading, the deterministic joins should probably be replaced by, or reported next to, probabilistic joins over the Monte Carlo draws. The spec's Monte Carlo requirement ("resampling dates within their intervals") implies the uncertainty reading for at least some records.
4. **Containment (minor).** Does A = B count as "inside" (non-strict containment)? The natural reading is yes (non-strict, inclusive whole years) [assumption]. Please confirm.
5. **Monte Carlo sampling distribution (not a join rule; noted for completeness).** The spec does not give the distribution. My default would be uniform over whole years in [t_low, t_high], with year 0 excluded via astronomical conversion [assumption]. Non-uniform priors (for example calibrated radiocarbon) would need a per-record distribution field, which §4 does not define.
6. **Circular-dating scope (minor).** §4 excludes a record from the test when "`dating_dependency` points to the other side of the join". Should this exclusion also cover transitive chains (A -> C -> B)? The acceptance criterion (A->B->A) needs cycle detection, which suggests yes. Please confirm that the scope is transitive.

Once items 1-3 are decided, the implementation fits within budget. The parameter choices (fraction denominator, lag inclusivity) should probably be recorded in `config/parameters.yaml` or as fixed decisions, which only the researcher can write.
